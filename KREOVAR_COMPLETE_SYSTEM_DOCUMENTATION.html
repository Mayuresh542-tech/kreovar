1. Executive Summary & Product Vision

Kreovar (internal codebase name veelox) is a full-stack, AI-native video creation and autonomous post-production platform designed specifically for high-retention short-form video (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts) and professional long-form content (Podcasts, Documentaries, Educational Explainers).

By parsing natural language user prompts (supporting English, Hindi, and Hinglish), Kreovar executes multi-step editorial workflows across visual framing, audio mastering, typography, B-roll insertion, and camera movement while strictly respecting timeline constraints, speaker identity, and user-specified boundaries.

2. High-Level System Architecture

Kreovar operates on a modern distributed architecture combining Cloudflare Edge Workers, Supabase PostgreSQL / Storage, High-Throughput Inference Engines (Groq & Gemini), and Client-Side Hardware-Accelerated Video Processing (WebCodecs & WebGPU).

4. The AI Video Editor — Deep Dive Engine

4.1 Project Data Model & Timeline Schema (model.ts)

A Kreovar project is represented as a strictly typed, immutable tree structure with independent tracks (main, overlay, image, captions, titles, audio) and clips possessing exact microsecond timestamps, speed properties, transforms (scale, rotation, opacity), and visual effects (brightness, contrast, saturation, blur, RGB offsets).

4.2 The 11-Stage Autonomous Orchestration Pipeline (sequentialEdit.ts)

To eliminate hallucinations and timeline corruption, Kreovar implements an 11-stage autonomous state machine:

  1. analyzeFootage: Analyzes raw duration, sampled video frames, and Whisper speech segments to establish required operations.
  2. applyCaptions: Applies kinetic captions matched to the video tone without re-transcribing existing audio.
  3. planMotionGraphics: Identifies key spoken concept moments requiring graphic illustration.
  4. applyMotionGraphics: Generates vector shapes (arrows, callouts, cards) with exact entry/exit keyframes.
  5. findBroll: Performs semantic search for real stock footage grounded in the transcript.
  6. applyBroll: Inserts B-roll onto overlay tracks, completely muted (volume: 0) with 0.25s crossfades.
  7. applyZooms: Identifies vocal emphasis peaks and applies punch-in zooms (1.18x–1.35x).
  8. applySoundEffects: Layers subtle SFX (0.08–0.25 volume) under transitions, zooms, and topic shifts.
  9. applyEffects: Applies filmic color grading, contrast, or lens blur ramps.
  10. applyRequestedEdits: Conversational execution handling all 28 direct editing operations.
  11. validateEdit: Automated linting verifying track integrity and zero-collision cuts.

4.3 Conversational Intent Parsing & Negative Constraints (editingIntent.ts)

Kreovar recognizes English, Hindi, and Hinglish negation keywords (don't, never, mat, nahi, bina, without). If a user says "add B-roll but don't add music or touch my voice", the engine strictly enforces zero modifications to audio tracks.

4.4 In-Place Mutation & Reconciler (inPlaceReconciler.ts)

When users issue follow-up instructions (e.g. "make captions yellow" or "remove the zoom at 5s"), the Reconciler directly updates existing clip properties in-place, eliminating duplicate tracks or ghost layers.

4.5 Semantic Phrase Timestamp Matcher (phraseMatcher.ts)

Bridges natural human phrasing with audio timestamps. Prompts like "when I talk about consistency, zoom in" are matched against Whisper word timestamps via fuzzy string alignment.

4.6 Dual Export Pipeline: Browser WebCodecs vs. Server FFmpeg

  • Browser WebCodecs (mediabunny): Encodes H.264/AAC MP4 directly on the user's GPU inside the browser canvas in real time. Zero server compute costs.
  • Server-Side FFmpeg: Generates high-fidelity transparent PNG sequences via Sharp and mixes multi-stream video/audio with non-destructive normalization (normalize=0).

6. Smart Footage & B-Roll System

Automatically maps speech entities to real portrait (9:16) stock video clips from Pexels and Pixabay, auto-trimming and fading them over speech without muting dialogue.

7. Dynamic Camera Zooms & Visual Pacing

Eliminates viewer drop-off by applying rhythmic jump-cut punch-ins (1.25x–1.35x) on key emphasis moments and pulling back out to wide framing every 2.5–3 seconds.

8. Sound Design & Audio Engine

Includes 10+ built-in sound effects (whooshes, pops, clicks, chimes, sub-drops) synchronized with camera movement and visual badges, mixed non-destructively.

9. AI Voice Studio & Text-to-Speech

Integrated with ElevenLabs and Voice AI for voiceover generation, voice cloning, and direct timeline placement.

10. Trend & Content Intelligence

Uses google-trends-api to discover breakout queries and generate high-hook viral scripts structured for maximum viewer retention.

11. Authentication, Database & Edge Deployment

Hosted globally on Cloudflare Workers (https://kreovar.online) with sub-15ms startup time, backed by Supabase PostgreSQL with strict Row Level Security (RLS).
